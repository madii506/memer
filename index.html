11 · faq
what is memer?

a meme maker for stocks. every stock is a green key in pixel shades, dressed by today's real move.

where do the numbers come from?

from tokenized versions of the stocks on solana (xStocks), read live from jupiter's public token data. if a number can't be read, memer shows a dash instead.

are these real stocks?

they're tokens on solana that track each stock's price. memer only reads the prices. it never buys, sells or holds anything.

is this financial advice?

no. it's memes. a green day is a crown, a red day is a beanie, and that's all it means.

can I use the memes?

yes. save them, post them, use them as your pfp. the stock logos belong to their companies; memer isn't tied to any of them.

what are xStocks?

tokens on solana that track a real stock or fund, issued by backed under the xStocks name. memer reads their live price, volume, traders and holders from jupiter.

when is a key green, red or flat?

the 24h move decides. inside ±0.3% it's a flat day. above that the crown comes out, below it the beanie goes on. the shades never come off.

do they trade when wall street is closed?

the tokens trade on solana around the clock, but the stock itself only trades in market hours, so moves outside them can be thin. tap any stock to see if wall street is open right now.
