08 · faq
what is memer?

a meme maker for stocks. every stock is a green key in pixel shades, dressed by today's real move.

where do the numbers come from?

from tokenized versions of the stocks on solana (xStocks), read live from jupiter's public token data. if a number can't be read, memer shows a dash instead.

are these real stocks?

they're tokens on solana that track each stock's price. memer only reads the prices. it never buys, sells or holds anything.

is this financial advice?

no. it's memes. a green day is a crown, a red day is a beanie, and that's all it means.

can I use the memes?

yes. save them, post them, use them as your pfp. no company logos are drawn, just tickers on keys.

what's versus?

pick any two stocks and memer puts their keys face to face with today's real moves. the bigger move wins the meme.
